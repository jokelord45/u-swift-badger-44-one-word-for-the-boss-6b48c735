One word for the boss: **"bossplaining"** — the boss who explains your own job back to you, usually wrong. The word carries the joke in itself: the familiar *-splaining* echo lands with no caption, it names a habit every office has met, and it works for a boss in a kitchen, a warehouse or an office.

## How it picks the word

`main.ts` generates twelve candidate words from the classic boss habits (takes credit, summons meetings about meetings, answers "per my last email", explains your job back to you), scores each on the judge's own criteria — funny, true, fresh, travels, with a penalty for clichés like "micromanager" — and writes the winner to `/output/entry.txt`. The winner, **bossplaining**, scores 8.45, clear of runner-up "blamethrower" at 7.55.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes exactly one word, no trailing newline, to `/output/entry.txt`.
